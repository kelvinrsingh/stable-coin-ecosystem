# Stablecoin infrastructure tokens: evidence and investment screening

Research cutoff: 29 September 2026, Australia/Melbourne. Research and paper testing only. Prices, market capitalisations and actual portfolio suitability were not established here. Mechanism confidence is not expected-return confidence. Sources were searched and official documents opened live; explorer checks blocked by HTTP 403 are explicitly recorded.

## Conclusions

1. Stablecoin adoption does not require any one volatile token. ETH, SOL, TRX, XRP and XLM supply chain-specific resources; users may have fees sponsored. Network necessity does not imply a large token balance per dollar settled.
2. ETH and SOL are useful first research priorities for settlement economics; TRX merits attention for resource economics with a separate concentration/governance review. This is a research priority, not a valuation ranking.
3. SKY offers a more direct documented relationship between protocol surplus, repurchases and staking distributions. LINK offers a documented revenue-to-reserve mechanism. Neither should be described as a shareholder dividend.
4. AAVE buyback documentation conflicts with later governance evidence of a pause. Do not assume the historical annual budget is current realised holder income.
5. Stablecoin-native fee chains challenge the claim that all growth must accrue to existing gas tokens. Arc and Tempo explicitly support fees paid in stablecoins.

## Critical evidence conflicts and limitations

### AAVE

The token documentation describes an annual $50m buyback budget beginning execution in April 2025. TokenLogic's 22 April 2026 governance post says transactions ceased on 19 April. A later community thread calls for restarting; a June comment mentions prospective Aavenomics 3.0 automated buybacks. Community enthusiasm and a reported future design are not an executed funding decision or a transaction. Until new funding updates and treasury purchase receipts are checked, use zero confirmed ongoing repurchases in the conservative case; treat resumed purchases as conditional upside. [Community thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/restart-aave-buy-backs/24936)

### SKY

The September 10 executive proposal specifies a 2,860,943.76 SKY burn and a 143,208,393 SKY farming vest over 90 days, as well as shorter buyback cycles. The proposal page uses conditional language; its presence alone does not establish execution. Its linked spell is `0x86d6CdD0D259AAAfb8134D47464b77743F50380B`. Explorer retrieval returned 403, so execution receipt was not verified here. Do not promote these precise parameter changes to executed fact. September 25 frontend material still describes treasury/buyback-funded rewards. [Executive proposal](https://vote.sky.money/executive/template-executive-vote-monthly-settlement-cycle-for-august-2026-treasury-management-function-parameter-updates-increase-mkr-sky-delayed-upgrade-penalty-adjust-allocator-vault-dc-iam-parameters-prime-agent-proxy-spells-september-10-2026), [September 25 explanation](https://sky.money/blog/what-staking-sky-unlocks)

### LINK

The reserve is observable at an official dashboard, but company-wide offchain revenue claims are issuer statements, not audited consolidated revenue verified here. Explorer retrieval for the linked reserve `0x9A709B7B69EA42D5eeb1ceBC48674C69E1569eC6` returned 403. No current reserve balance or realised trailing-12-month buys is asserted. A reserve balance increase also needs attribution: external customer payments, treasury transfers and withdrawals must be distinguished. [Reserve dashboard](https://metrics.chain.link/reserve)

## Competitive chains and new-token supply risk

- **Arc:** Circle announced public mainnet launch September 16, 2026, with USDC gas and a permissioned validator set. This is a dated issuer announcement, not independent verification of institutional activity or production transaction economics. Do not infer an investable ARC token from launch. For this screen the investable comparison is Circle equity, subject to the equity team's work. [Announcement](https://www.circle.com/pressroom/circle-launches-arc-mainnet-an-economic-operating-system-for-the-internet)
- **Tempo:** official website and docs describe mainnet and USD-stablecoin fees, with no volatile gas token needed. Payment adoption can benefit service operators without generating demand for an ETH/SOL-style volatile gas asset. No public-token investment established in this pass. [Website](https://tempo.xyz/), [Docs](https://docs.tempo.xyz/)
- **Plasma/XPL:** official allocation gives 10bn initial supply; team and investors each receive 25%. One-third of each allocation unlocks after a one-year mainnet-beta cliff. With the announced September 25, 2025 launch, the scheduled cliff is September 25, 2026—already past the research cutoff's preceding weekend. Combined scheduled amount is approximately 1.667bn XPL (5bn / 3); actual transfers not verified. Ecosystem releases continue separately. Inflation is described as conditional on external validators/delegation, not established as active here. Watch realised fee capture, unlock receipts and incentive-free retained liquidity before treating stablecoin deposits as investment merit. [Allocation](https://www.plasma.org/company/blog/xpl-the-public-sale-and-its-role-in-the-plasma-ecosystem), [Launch](https://www.plasma.org/company/blog/plasma-mainnet-beta-and-xpl)

## Paper-screen decision rules (analyst-designed, not empirical return forecasts)

All candidates remain research-only and unpriced. No immediate paper entry until timestamped price, circulating/total supply, transaction costs and market liquidity are recorded. Use a 3–5 year thesis horizon with quarterly review, first due December 29, 2026, plus event-triggered review for exploits, governance changes and major unlocks.

| Candidate | Evidence required before paper entry | Thesis invalidation / review trigger |
|---|---|---|
| ETH | 90-day stablecoin-attributable gas and L2 settlement data; net issuance; price scenario that works without expanding valuation multiple | Two consecutive quarters of adoption growth with falling retained settlement economics requires model reset; material security failure triggers immediate review |
| SOL | Separate base burns, priority fees, staking issuance and incentives; demonstrate retained real payment users; model price using projected supply | Fee growth driven mainly by temporary speculation/incentives; stablecoin usage grows while economic benefit per diluted token deteriorates |
| TRX | Reconcile stablecoin usage with burn, issuance and resource rental economics; concentration and issuer dependency review | Material issuer withdrawal, sustained net dilution without compensating fee growth, or resource governance changes breaking model |
| LINK | Reconcile 90-day reserve additions to verifiable paid services and releases; do not equate integration announcements with sales | Supply releases persistently dominate economically funded purchases; reserve withdrawals or nontransparent revenue attribution |
| AAVE | Verify buyback restart receipts or value without buybacks; reconcile losses, recovery proceeds and unfunded obligations | New bad debt, treasury shortfall, governance redirects value away from tokenholders; no restart is a failed catalyst, not automatic protocol failure |
| SKY | Verify current spell execution and actual net token retirement/distribution; net surplus after savings rewards, credit costs and operating costs | Credit loss/depeg, negative sustainable surplus, holder benefit materially reduced by governance, or buys funded by unsustainable balance-sheet depletion |
| XRP/XLM | Demonstrate meaningful fee/reserve/bridge inventory demand relative to float; compare against supply distributions | Growth stays in issued stablecoins with negligible native-token economic demand; neither asset gets an entry solely on payment partnerships |
| XPL | Confirm cliff and continuing unlocks; prove activity retention after incentives, live validator economics and effective fees | Liquidity exits when incentives expire; unlock supply overwhelms plausible economic demand; low/no-fee transfers fail to fund security sustainably |

For comparability, use fully diluted projections and three cases: lower fee/take-rate and faster supply release; adoption growth with constant valuation multiple; adoption plus durable monetisation improvement. Do not apply issuer reserve-income multiples to gas tokens. Do not count staking issuance both as holder income and as zero-cost dilution.

## Data discipline

No chain-level stablecoin supply share or adjusted payment-volume totals are asserted: comparable primary data were not extracted in this subtask. Obtain consistent end-of-day snapshots, classify native versus bridged liabilities, exclude self-transfers and mint/burn movements from payment comparisons, and record methodology. USD settlement value is neither fee revenue nor the amount of token inventory users need. Market-attractiveness rankings require actual prices; assigning one from these mechanisms alone would be false precision.
