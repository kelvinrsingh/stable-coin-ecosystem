Research cutoff: 29 September 2026, Australia/Melbourne. USD unless specified. Research and paper-watchlist input only; no orders. Sources below were discovered through broad official-domain searches, then opened; financial tables were checked against SEC filings where available. Latest completed reporting periods found: calendar Q2 2026; Visa calls the same June-ended quarter fiscal Q3. These are historical financials, not September live balances or prices.

## Discovery and verification record

Discovery covered issuers, distributors, card networks, payment platforms and a private issuer comparator. Searches surfaced Circle and Coinbase earnings releases/10-Qs, Visa and Mastercard June-quarter results and infrastructure announcements, PayPal's 10-Q, and Tether's reserve report. The strongest evidence is filed financial data; issuer announcements establish what management reports, not independently proven adoption or future economics. No live quote, AUD/USD rate or complete current diluted-capitalisation snapshot was acquired. Therefore this packet ranks research relevance, not expected stock returns.

## Numerical scenarios: economics, not price targets

Circle's June 2026 filing models the following **12-month changes**, holding June-end circulation and reserve allocation constant, relative to June's 3.49% average yield. This is the issuer's mechanical sensitivity, not a forecast:

| Rate scenario | Reserve income change | Distribution/transaction cost change | Calculated net change before other costs/tax |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear: -100bp | -$737m | -$360m | -$377m |
| Base: unchanged | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Bull: +100bp | +$737m | +$360m | +$377m |

Circle also reported $289m quarterly revenue less distribution costs and 268.637m weighted-average diluted shares. Outstanding RSUs were 14.635m. H1 operating cash flow included $222m ARC token-sale proceeds; these should not be extrapolated as recurring reserve earnings. [Circle 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1876042/000187604226000248/crcl-20260630.htm)

**Independent calculation:** at a hypothetical 3.5% yield, a cut to 2.5% requires 40% more average reserves merely to preserve gross interest revenue: 3.5/2.5 - 1. This assumes identical reserve composition and ignores fees, distribution and expenses. Supply growth alone is insufficient.

Valuation model to use for CRCL: forecast average supply × earned reserve yield; subtract contractual distribution and transaction costs; add independently evidenced service revenue; subtract cash expenses, recurring economic stock compensation, capitalised development, tax and working-capital needs. Discount owner cash flows; add only unrestricted corporate net cash; divide by fully diluted shares, modelling future awards. Never add stablecoin backing reserves as free equity cash.

For COIN, use a sum of parts: stablecoin economics **net of rewards and allocated costs**, mid-cycle trading, custody/subscriptions, and network services. Exclude mark-to-market gains from recurring earnings. The 10-Q moved corporate stablecoin income out of stablecoin revenue in 2026 and restated prior comparisons; mixing original 2025 figures with 2026 definitions creates false growth. [Coinbase 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1679788/000167978826000088/coin-20260630.htm)

For V/MA/PYPL, model the core business first. Add stablecoin revenue only with substantiated volume × net take-rate estimates; subtract any displaced card/cross-border income and implementation costs. Stress capital allocation, acquisitions and per-share dilution. No stablecoin-specific price target is defensible from the reviewed disclosures.

## Three-year CRCL valuation experiment — assumptions, not a fair-value recommendation

All scenario settings below are analyst judgments. Initial circulation is $73.3bn; initial annual service revenue is $136m; initial annual GAAP operating expenses are $1,016m ($254m quarterly × 4). The 268.637m Q2 weighted-average diluted-share count is a **proxy, not current capitalisation**. The illustrative $85.60 reference is a parent-reported Alpaca IEX last trade at 2026-09-28T20:07:14Z (after-hours); it is not an execution quote and was not independently retrieved by this subtask.

| Assumption | Bear | Base | Bull |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Supply annual growth | 10% | 25% | 40% |
| Reserve yield | 2% | 3% | 4% |
| Reserve income retained after distribution | 35% | 40% | 45% |
| Service revenue annual growth | 10% | 20% | 30% |
| GAAP operating expense annual growth | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| Annual share dilution | 4% | 3% | 2% |
| Terminal earnings multiple | 20×, only if profitable | 25× | 30× |

Formula: year-three average earning reserves = mean(year-two ending supply, year-three ending supply), a linear growth approximation within year three; retained reserve income = average earning reserves × yield × retention; add service revenue grown for three years and subtract operating expenses grown for three years. Apply 25% tax only to positive EBIT; loss cases receive no assumed tax credit. Divide by the diluted-share proxy grown for three years. Multiply positive EPS by the selected terminal multiple. No net cash adjustment, dividends, FX, financing income/expense or intra-period cash distributions are assumed.

| Year-three result | Bear | Base | Bull |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ending circulation ($bn) | 97.56 | 143.16 | 201.14 |
| Average earning reserves ($bn) | 93.13 | 128.85 | 172.40 |
| Retained reserve income ($m) | 651.9 | 1,546.2 | 3,103.2 |
| Service revenue ($m) | 181.0 | 235.0 | 298.8 |
| GAAP operating expenses ($m) | 1,352.3 | 1,545.2 | 1,755.6 |
| EBIT ($m) | -519.4 | 236.0 | 1,646.4 |
| Net income proxy ($m) | -519.4 | 177.0 | 1,234.8 |
| Diluted-share proxy (m) | 302.18 | 293.55 | 285.08 |
| EPS proxy ($) | -1.72 | 0.60 | 4.33 |
| Terminal value proxy ($/share) | Not meaningful: loss | 15.07 | 129.94 |
| Annualised price return from $85.60 | Not computable with P/E | -44.0% | 14.9% |

The bear case is loss-making: multiplying negative EPS by a positive P/E does not produce a valid investment price. A loss-making company could retain option, asset or recovery value; this model does not estimate it. A blank target is not a zero target.

**Critical limitations:** average earning reserves use the midpoint of year-two and year-three ending supply; actual daily balance paths can differ from this linear approximation. Service revenue is treated as contribution before shared operating expenses, with no separate service cost-of-revenue assumption. Retention is a simplified constant rather than the actual changing contractual payout schedule. GAAP operating expenses contain stock compensation whose future amount may vary with share price, awards and vesting; modelling both expenses and share dilution must be reconciled to actual awards. EPS is not free cash flow: capitalised development, working capital and nonrecurring token proceeds require separate treatment. Multiples, rates and growth are correlated in reality; this experiment assigns no probabilities. The illustrative returns omit AUD/USD movements, tax, fees and slippage.

Interpretation: the chosen base assumptions imply a much lower terminal value than the illustrative mark; the chosen bull assumptions imply approximately 14.9% annualised price appreciation. This diagnoses how demanding the embedded economics could be under these assumptions, **not whether CRCL is fairly valued today**. Before using a target, replace proxy share counts and average-balance approximations, reconcile recurring costs and contracts, and validate the current price.
