# Stablecoin investment ecosystem — discovery packet

Date: 2026-09-29 Australia/Melbourne. Horizon: 3–5 years. Audience: Kelvin Singh. Status: discovered-but-unverified; verification in separate report. Research only; no orders.

## Intent and questions
Map stablecoin creation, reserves, distribution, settlement, interoperability, lending and payments. Identify whether usage creates revenue that reaches shareholders or tokenholders, then evaluate valuation, downside and AUD outcomes. Compare CRCL/COIN/V/MA/PYPL; ETH/SOL/TRX/LINK/AAVE/SKY; XRP/XLM and private issuers/payment rails as comparators.

## Query expansion
Stablecoin, payment stablecoin, digital dollar, USDC/USDT/USDS/GHO/PYUSD/RLUSD, synthetic dollar/USDe; reserve yield, float, take rate, distribution payments, redemption, supply net issuance, token burn, buyback, dilution, unlocks; tokenised deposits, money-market funds, real-world assets, oracles and burn-and-mint interoperability. Exclude airdrop promotion, referral yields and unsupported price targets.

## Early hypotheses (not conclusions)
Reserve income and distribution could capture more than low-fee settlement tokens. Growing transfer value may not generate proportionate fees. Native issuer chains could change capture. Lending yields and synthetic-dollar funding returns have additional risks; stablecoin ownership is not issuer equity.

## Gaps and verification plan
Current prices/FDV, quarter-end versus live supply, economic-activity methodology, exact enacted-law effective dates, implemented buyback status, token dilution and minority-holder entitlements need verification. Search snippets are insufficient. Open primary sources, retain dates and units, calculate scenarios transparently, and identify undisclosed attribution rather than infer it. Distinguish attestations from full audits and announcements from deployments.
