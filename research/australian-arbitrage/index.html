Why a displayed edge can disappear

The synthetic fixture starts with opposing prices of 2.10 and 2.05. With approximately AUD 1,000 of capital and AUD 1 of allocated cost, its worst normal-settlement result is AUD 36.35. The replay assumes the first leg has filled and tests the remaining hedge at the original stake.

These are constructed tests, not observed market behaviour or successful betting examples. Actual fills are never inferred from displayed prices.

What the scanner checks

  • Fixed-odds arbitrage: exhaustive two- or three-outcome markets, equal-payout sizing, cent rounding and costs.
  • Back/lay arbitrage: exchange commission, lay liability, hedge capacity and capital at each venue.
  • Evidence: source timestamps, quote age, outcome completeness, licensing review and settlement compatibility.
  • Execution exposure: observed 2/5/15/30-second replay when data exists, plus assumed partial fills, adverse price moves and one-sided voids.
  • Audit trail: raw snapshots, rejected comparisons, missing evidence, first-ingestion times and the exact configuration for each run.

Defaults: AUD 1,000 capital per comparison, AUD 1,000 assumed funds per venue and AUD 1 illustrative allocated cost. These are adjustable assumptions. Comparisons share prices and capital; their profits must not be added.

A positive calculation with missing limits or rules stays blocked. The term paper_candidate means the configured assumptions pass; it does not mean a trade was accepted or is guaranteed.

Use the package

Open README.md for the complete setup, formulas, evidence schema, scope and sources. Python 3.10+ is needed only to run new calculations; there are no third-party Python dependencies.

python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python3 -m aus_arb run data/public_nrl_2026-09-29.json --out results/my_scan

To collect a new AU snapshot, obtain your own The Odds API key, set ODDS_API_KEY locally, then run:

python3 -m aus_arb collect --sport rugbyleague_nrl --out results/my_collection

This makes one read-only request. Repeated collection is opt-in, consumes provider quota and requires the process to stay running. Published odds alone do not supply all the account and settlement evidence needed for a qualifying paper candidate.
